# Privacy policy

CSES Pacha Tools collects no personal data and sends nothing to its author or to any third party.

- It runs only on `https://cses.fi/problemset*` pages.
- Its settings (view, filter, team, recent teammates) are stored in your browser's `localStorage` for cses.fi and never leave your computer.
- Its only network requests go to cses.fi, with your existing CSES session: your teammates' statistics pages, your CSES friends page (for suggestions) and, only when you pick "Look up on CSES", a request that adds that user as a CSES friend to find their id.
- Teammate suggestions also use a list of public CSES usernames, user ids and solved counts (users with 5+ solved tasks, from the CSES ranking) bundled inside the extension; searching it happens on your computer.
- It has no analytics, ads or remote code.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/pacha2880/cses-pacha-tools/issues.
