# Privacy policy

CSES Pacha Tools collects no personal data and sends nothing to its author or to any third party.

- It runs only on `https://cses.fi/problemset*` pages.
- Its settings (view, "Hide solved", the team box) are stored in your browser's `localStorage` for cses.fi and never leave your computer.
- Its only network requests go to cses.fi, with your existing CSES session: your teammates' public statistics pages and, when you type a username in the team box, your CSES friends page (it may add that user as a CSES friend to look up their id).
- It has no analytics, ads or remote code.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/pacha2880/cses-pacha-tools/issues.
